# Bookshare search and availability

Official help checked October 30, 2026. Use these as navigation guidance;
inspect the current interface rather than assuming labels or features persist.

- [Finding books](https://www.bookshare.org/help-and-learning-articles/how-do-i-find-books):
  basic search uses title, author, ISBN, and synopsis. Advanced Search has a
  separate Full Text field and filters; multiple fields combine with AND.
- [Books available to a member](https://www.bookshare.org/help-and-learning-articles/what-books-are-available-to-me):
  signed-in results reflect the member's country. A public listing is not proof
  that the current account can download it.
- [Available formats](https://www.bookshare.org/help-and-learning-articles/what-format-are-bookshare-books-available-in):
  text choices include EPUB, Word, and DAISY. Inspect the specific book's menu;
  do not assume all listed formats are available for every title.

For an uncertain title, try distinctive title words plus author, then a verified
print or ebook ISBN. Series titles can hide volume differences; verify the
reading coverage and ISBN against a publisher source if needed. Check active
filters and alternate records before reporting “not found in these searches.”
Do not turn that result into a claim that Bookshare never carries the work.

A lower-quality or older scan may still be useful when it is the only edition,
but disclose known issues. For art research, verify whether illustrations and
captions are present rather than assuming a text download includes every image.
